# Content rules for PM1 lessons

## What we keep from the uploaded teacher guide
- 60-minute scenario with explicit phases.
- Observable learning result.
- Terms explained through examples rather than memorized definitions.
- One main case plus a reserve case.
- Controlled comparisons: keep parameters fixed and change one factor.
- Separate observation, interpretation, and unsupported assertion.
- State the boundary of every professional conclusion.
- Diagnostic questions check understanding before grading.
- Theory does not pretend to be independent practical evidence.

## What we keep from the uploaded P001 presentation
- 15-slide rhythm as a strong default, not a hard limit for every lesson.
- Fixed cover role.
- Recurrent lesson/module context.
- Visible slide number.
- Frequent oral interaction.
- “why / because”, A/B comparison, teacher demo, timed search, mini-debate, professional reformulation, checklist, summary.

## What we remove from the master
Service/generated text must never appear on student-facing slides:
- “Цель: ...”
- “Связать действие...”
- “Визуалы повторно использованы...”
- placeholder labels like “Признак 1: что можно увидеть и проверить”
- generic “Вариант А/Б” when no real content is supplied.

These belong in metadata/teacher notes or must be replaced by actual lesson content.
