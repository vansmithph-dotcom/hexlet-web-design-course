## Photo-first rule
- Slides 2–14: one meaningful photograph per slide.
- Prefer real photographic scenes of a design/computer classroom, workstation, cables, equipment, scanner, passages, liquids, lighting, posture, and safe/unsafe organization.
- Do **not** replace a photograph with a decorative schematic.
- Use diagrams only when a diagram is genuinely necessary to explain sequence, causality, or interface logic better than a photograph.
- A/B comparison slides should use photographic comparison whenever possible.
- Observation tasks should use a full photographic case that students can inspect.
- Avoid repeated photos and repeated compositions in the final production set.
- Slide 15 (summary) may be text/graphic only.
